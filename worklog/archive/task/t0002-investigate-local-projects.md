# Investigate local projects

## Outcome

Assess whether n0002 describes most recorded worklog activity, where its expected paths diverge from practice, and which work kinds it does not assess.

## Completion criteria

- Survey representative worklog projects and classify their recorded tasks.
- Identify recurring mistakes and divergences for each n0002 path.
- Identify work kinds that n0002 does not assess adequately.
- Record accepted implications in n0002 and separate future or out-of-scope implications.

## Result

- The survey covered nine worklog projects, including 240 tasks and 65 specs.
- n0002 covered 76.7% of tasks at a coarse category level but did not adequately describe their execution.
- Recurring divergences included speculative or stale specs, incomplete path coverage, missing `modifies`, stale task state, late task creation, inadequate verification, omitted spec write-back, and stale markers.
- Investigation and review were the largest uncovered work kind. Other gaps included blocked or negative work, multi-session work, human-judgment verification, mixed-purpose work, projects outside worklog, and documentation or reporting deliverables.
- n0002 now includes a combined investigation-or-review path and objective conditions for chores or urgent fixes to leave worklog entities untouched. Spec-only work is treated as an outcome of its underlying purpose rather than a separate path.
- Autonomy and verification modes remain future work in n0002; blocked-work and mixed-purpose routing remain outside its scope.
