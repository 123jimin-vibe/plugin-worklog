# On writing specs and tasks

## General

- Important aspects, ordered by relative importance: accuracy >= lack of ambiguity > readability > conciseness
- Prefer titles with specificity.
  - Avoid umbrella words such as "governance", "improvements", or "miscellaneous".
- Prefer the user's terminology.
  - Do not silently broaden or reinterpret it.
  - You MAY ask if there's a clearly better alternative.
- Prefer one fact, rule, or action per statement.
- Remove narration, repeated context, and conclusions already implied elsewhere.
- Record current state and intent, not the conversation and history that produced it.
- Reference another item instead of restating it.
